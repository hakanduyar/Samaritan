# Roadmap

These are open tasks, not completed features.

1. **Navigation effort and reading stops:** compare small gestures, bursts, held keys, release and reverse without losing smoothness. Avoid long stationary scroll intervals and unintended departure from a reading surface.
2. **Ambient coverage and forward depth:** extend restrained background motion across the view and later route depths; keep focused photos stable and prevent sudden object appearances.
3. **Opening and typography:** compare legibility at laptop and portrait sizes; clearly distinguish a cinematic sequence from real loading progress.
4. **Whole-flow browser checks:** inspect every approach, hold, departure and endpoint using wheel, touch and keyboard; test visibility changes and reduced-motion behavior.
5. **Rendering budget:** measure frame times and memory on representative devices, especially fine distant lines and transparency.
6. **Accessibility:** review keyboard discoverability, reduced-motion alternatives and nonvisual access before claiming broad accessibility.
