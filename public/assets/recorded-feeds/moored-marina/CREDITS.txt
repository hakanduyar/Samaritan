Changes made (derivative material)
  - Cut source seconds 4-12.
  - Global shore translation stabilization (vidstab, tripod reference frame 1,
    rotation disabled); no zoom, no synthetic fill, no reverse, no fades.
  - Fixed common crop 1248x702 at offset (16, 9), then scaled to 960x540;
    audio removed; encoded as H.264 (CRF 25) with faststart.
  - Natural small boat bob and water motion are retained.
  - Extracted a still frame as the poster.
  - tracking.json is original work of this project: two hand-authored, constant
    normalized boxes (top-left origin) around the hull and cabin of two central
    moored boats, excluding reflections and thin antenna tips. They are
    coarse bounds, not live inference or exact physical detection.
