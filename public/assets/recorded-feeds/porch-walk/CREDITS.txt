Source

  (inward view of a porch and door), file:
  https://mevadata-public-01.s3.amazonaws.com/drops-123-r13/2018-03-07/16/2018-03-07.16-50-00.16-55-00.bus.G506.r13.avi

Changes made (derivative material)
  - Cut a 16-second excerpt in native time, forward only (no reverse, no
    retiming, no crossfade).
  - Fixed crop of the 1920x1072 native frame (x 0, y 240, width 1440, height
    810), then scaled to 960x540 square pixels.
  - Removed audio and container metadata; re-encoded as H.264 MP4, 30 fps,
    with timestamps starting at 0.
  - Extracted a still frame as the poster.
  - tracking.json is original work of this project: a hand-authored head
    bounding box (normalized, top-left origin) with explicit invisible keys
    before entry, while hidden behind the porch column and after exit. It is
    not output of live inference and not an identification of any person. It
    is offered under CC BY 4.0.
